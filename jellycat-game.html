How to Play the Jellycat Game

You're shown a black silhouette of a Jellycat plush toy. Your job is to guess which Jellycat it is by typing the name into the search box. You get 3 guesses per round — after your first wrong guess, a category hint is revealed to help you out.

Get it right and keep your streak going! Get it wrong and the round ends — but you'll see the colour reveal and can try again straight away.
