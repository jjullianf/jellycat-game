How Does the Jellycat Quiz Work?

Each round you see a black silhouette of a Jellycat plush toy. You have 3 attempts to guess the correct name. After your first wrong guess, you get a category hint — for example "Amuseables — Food" or "Animals — Bunny". After your second wrong guess you get one final chance before the answer is revealed.

The quiz is endless — keep going as long as you can and try to beat your personal streak record!

How Many Jellycats Are in the Quiz?

The quiz currently features 81 Jellycats across all major categories — animals, food, sports, bunnies, dragons and more. The order is randomised every time so no two games are the same. New Jellycats are added regularly!

Tips for the Quiz

🐢 Start with the shape — long ears usually means a bunny, round shapes often mean Amuseables Food items.

💡 Use the category hint wisely — it narrows down the options significantly.

🔥 Focus on building your streak — the longer you go without a miss, the better your score!
